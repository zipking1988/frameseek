## Material limits

The real index remains sparse and caption-only. Local ranking is lexical with
temporal exploration, not a measured semantic/object-tracking index. Verification
derives criteria using the configured VLM when explicitly sent, so parser tests
cannot prove that all intended criteria or visual facts are recognized correctly.
No fresh paid/live recognition test was performed. Recall, distractor rejection
and temporal localization on annotated real clips remain unaccepted. Do not
promise all occurrences, global absence, identity continuity or reliable totals.
